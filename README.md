# Ramp ID Trainer

Plane ID training for ramp agents and line techs: business jets and Boeing/Airbus airliners.

- **Quick lookup**: fuel (single-point vs overwing), lav service, and ramp boxes by wingspan
- **Test yourself**: quiz on blueprint-style side views
- **Prince's test**: quiz on real photos

Photos are from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org) under free licenses (public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA). Each photo's author and license are credited in the app.

Fuel and lav locations are a quick reference only. Always follow the crew, the aircraft manual and your FBO's procedures.
